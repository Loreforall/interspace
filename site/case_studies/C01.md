# C01 — Centered impact / shell energy transport

**Evidence status:** ARCHIVED

## Research question and setup
Reference impact; track contact penetration, projectile kinetic energy, structural energy and energy closure.

## Governing model
`m r¨ = Fspring + Fdamp + Fcontact; E0 = Kprojectile + Kshell + U + Q + Econtact + residual`

## Implementation references
hemisphere.py, surface_contact.py, transport3d.py

## Numerical outputs / evaluation
Peak overlap; projectile rebound; peak shell kinetic energy; residual magnitude.

## Data provenance
Existing archived dataset: `../data/stage12_baseline.csv`. These are prior solver outputs, not newly generated runs.

## Validity and limitations
Mesh/timestep sensitivity and conservation checks; contact is penalty-based, not experimentally calibrated.

**Scope:** computational demonstration, not experimentally validated impact-protection hardware.
