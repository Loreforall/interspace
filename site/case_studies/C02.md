# C02 — Off-axis versus polar impact

**Evidence status:** ARCHIVED

## Research question and setup
Compare impact location and asymmetric load transfer; polar and off-axis histories are stored.

## Governing model
`Fcontact = kc max(0,δ)n; τ = r × F; Δp = ∫F dt`

## Implementation references
stage10_extended.py, surface_contact.py

## Numerical outputs / evaluation
Contact overlap; peak voltage; resistor power; energy residual.

## Data provenance

## Validity and limitations

**Scope:** computational demonstration, not experimentally validated impact-protection hardware.
